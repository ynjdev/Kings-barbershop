Good to know

Frequently asked

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking guarantees your slot with your barber. That matters most from Thursday to Saturday, when chairs fill fastest.

What's the difference between Fresha and WhatsApp?

Choosing a date and time on Fresha books you in straight away, with a confirmation and a reminder. A WhatsApp request is booked once we reply with your time.

What if I want a different barber next time?

No problem. Just pick a different barber when you book. Most clients stick with one barber, but there's no lock-in.

What if I'm not happy with my cut?

Before you leave the chair, your barber checks the finished cut with you in the mirror. If anything isn't right, it gets fixed there and then, at no extra cost.

Do you take card?

Yes. We accept card and SnapScan, as well as cash.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Use the link in your Fresha confirmation, or message us on WhatsApp at least a few hours ahead, and we'll move your slot at no charge.
