Good to know

Frequently asked

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking on WhatsApp guarantees your slot with your barber — especially Thursday to Saturday when chairs fill fastest.

What if I want a different barber next time?

No problem — just say so in your WhatsApp message. Most clients stick with one barber, but there's no lock-in.

What if I'm not happy with my cut?

Before you leave the chair, your barber checks the finished cut with you in the mirror. If anything isn't right, it gets fixed there and then, at no extra cost.

Do you take card?

Yes — card and SnapScan are both accepted, alongside cash.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Message the same WhatsApp number at least a few hours ahead and we'll move your slot — no charge.
