The story

One standard, every chair

Over a decade of cutting has taught Jermaine what a great cut takes: a proper consultation, time to get the details right, and a finish that still looks sharp weeks later.

He opened Kings to hold every cut to that standard, in his chair and his team's. Whether it's your first visit or your fiftieth, you get the same care.
