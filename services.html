Standing Chair

R650 per month

4 Signature Cuts a month, normally R720

  • Same barber, same day and time every week
  • Free lineup between cuts
  • First pick of Thursday to Saturday slots
How it works
  • Your 4 cuts are for the calendar month and don't roll over.
  • If your slot day falls five times in a month, the fifth cut is at the normal price.
  • To move a slot, message us at least 4 hours before. Later than that, it counts as used.
  • One missed slot a month is on us. A second missed slot in the same month counts as a used cut.
  • If your barber is off, you rebook with him that week and keep the cut. You only move to another barber if you ask to.
  • No contract. Cancel any month and it ends when your paid month does.
